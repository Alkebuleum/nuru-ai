Recovery Guardians are trusted contacts — people or devices you designate — who may assist in a wallet recovery process under specific contractual conditions defined by your smart account's on-chain logic. Recovery Guardians are not a guaranteed recovery mechanism. Nuru makes no representation, warranty, or promise that the Guardian system will successfully recover your wallet under any circumstance. The Guardian feature is a supplementary safeguard, not a replacement for proper key security. Your first and most important line of defense is protecting your master key. Do not rely on guardians as a substitute for secure key management.
